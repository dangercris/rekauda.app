# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Parents and guardians of a school class (typically 30 to 40 families). They open the link from the class WhatsApp group on a phone, usually in daylight and between other tasks. Many are not technical. Some pay for two siblings or for a grandparent and a teen at once.

The class representative (the site owner) reviews registrations in `/admin`, confirms each transfer against the bank and marks it paid.

## Product Purpose

A single place for every collection this class runs (costumes, trips, gifts, graduation). For each collection a family must be able to: see what they owe and by when, pay by Deuna or by bank transfer, register the payment with the receipt in under two minutes, and later check its status with a short code. The class can see overall progress without exposing private data.

Success: every family pays before the deadline without asking the representative in WhatsApp what the account number is, how much it is, or whether their payment arrived.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, calm, precise. It handles other people's money, so it must feel like a well-made payment product, not a school flyer. Warm enough to belong to a school community; never cute, never loud. Each collection brings its own color, but the site is clearly not an official school site.

## Anti-references

- School flyers and Canva posters: clip art, many colors, centered everything.
- Generic SaaS landing pages: hero metrics, gradient text, glass cards, identical icon-card grids.
- Bank web portals: dense, grey, intimidating forms.
- Anything that imitates the official site or logo of a school.

## Design Principles

1. **Two minutes, one thumb.** Every screen is designed for a phone held in one hand; the next action is always reachable at the bottom.
2. **Money needs certainty.** Amounts, account data and status are shown exactly, with copy buttons and confirmation, never ambiguous.
3. **One step at a time.** Paying, registering and checking status are separate, guided moments; nothing asks for information before it is needed.
4. **Private by default.** Public views show first name and surname initial only; receipts and phones never leave the admin.
5. **Reusable, not rebuilt.** Each new collection is configuration (title, options, prices, deadline), never a new page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Touch targets at least 44 px. Body and inputs at 16 px minimum (also prevents iOS zoom). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-color-scheme`. Status is never conveyed by color alone (text label plus icon). Spanish (Ecuador) copy, neutral register, no regional slang.
